# SEO research: Teachable alternative for building courses with AI agents (2026-10-20)

Backlog H30. Comparison stage, no page today.

## Verdict

Defer. Don't build a comparison page yet.

## Evidence

- Volume (SE Ranking, US): "teachable alternative" doesn't make the top 15 ideas by volume (floor 260/mo). The demand sits on "thinkific vs teachable" (320), "teachable vs kajabi" (260), "kajabi alternatives" (260) and head terms like "best online course platforms" (480, KD 31).
- Google top 10 for "teachable alternative" and "teachable alternative for AI course creation": listicles from course platforms (Podia, Circle, systeme.io, TrainerCentral, GetResponse, AppSumo) plus Reddit threads. The promise is price, 0% fees, and selling features. No result talks about agents or adaptive learning.
- PAA: "What is the best AI tool for course creation?" This maps to /ai-course-builder (live exp-001), not a comparison page.
- AI citations (Step 1b, Oct 19): both Google AI Mode and ChatGPT cite coursebox.ai for the frozen comparison prompt. We're cited by neither.

## Why defer

Searchers for "Teachable alternative" want cheaper selling platforms. Graspful's real answer is a different job (agent-built adaptive courses), so a comparison page would rank for the wrong promise. Revisit if Search Console shows any "alternative" or "vs" query, or if a signal asks for an agent-first Teachable replacement.
